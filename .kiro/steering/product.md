# Product Steering: Tendly

## Vision & Purpose
Tendly bridges the gap between complex government procurement systems and small business owners. The goal is to democratize access to public sector revenue by simplifying discovery, qualification, and the initial response phase.

## User Personas
* **The Newbie (First-time Entrepreneur):** Needs heavy guidance on registrations (UEI, CAGE codes) and "plain English" contract summaries.
* **The Scaler (Established SMB):** Needs high-signal filtering to find "set-aside" contracts (Women-owned, Veteran-owned, etc.) quickly.
* **The Consultant:** Manages multiple clients; needs a dashboard to track bid statuses and deadlines.

## Core Features & Workflows
1.  **Contract Aggregator:** Real-time ingestion from SAM.gov, grants.gov, and regional portals.
2.  **Smart Match Engine:** Vector-based matching between a user's business profile (NAICS codes, past performance) and active solicitations.
3.  **The "Tender Readiness" Score:** A checklist-driven UI that evaluates if a business is legally and operationally ready to bid.
4.  **AI Bid Drafter:** A generative UI component that pulls business context to draft initial Capability Statements.

## Success Metrics (KPIs)
* **Match Accuracy:** Percentage of recommended contracts marked as "Relevant" by users.
* **Time-to-Discovery:** Reduction in time spent searching versus legacy government portals.
* **Submission Rate:** Number of users who progress from "Viewing" to "Drafting" a response.
